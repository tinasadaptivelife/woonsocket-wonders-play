Igloo and Snowball Fight

Saturday afternoon, fresh snow in the backyard. Uncle Joe cuts the snow blocks, Dad shows Clara how to set them, and Bernie supervises. When the igloo is done, the grown-ups build a fort.

Difficulty

How to play

  • Build the igloo: a snow block slides along the top. Press the space bar (or Drop the block, or click) when it is over the glowing spot.
  • Snowball fight: left and right arrows aim lower or higher. Hold the space bar (or Hold to throw) for power, then let go. Or point and hold the mouse.
  • Duck into the igloo with the down arrow or the Duck button when Dad or Uncle Joe winds up. Press it again to come back out.
  • First to 7 hits wins. Bernie sometimes catches a snowball.

Dad's and Uncle Joe's lines are written for the game, not from the show. Their voices are AI-generated.

Good game!
