Games on the Parlor TV

It is 1979, the radiator is ticking, and the popcorn is a little too salty. Clara pulls the joystick close to the TV. Pick a game.

Difficulty
Start at level

Every game has 5 levels. Each level is a little faster and trickier. Clear one to go on to the next.

Paddle Ball

Bounce the ball past the other paddle. First to 7 points.

Mill Bricks

Bounce the ball up and knock every brick out of the old mill wall.

Snowflake Storm

Rows of snowflakes drift down toward the porch. Burst them all with snowballs before they land.

Whiskers Pinball

A river pinball table. Bounce off the river stones, pick all four enchanted raspberries, and splash into Whiskers' moon pool. Pick every raspberry to go up a level.

Franky's Candy Maze

Collect every sugar drop between the candy jars in Bonbons du Temps Passé. Wisps of forgetting fog drift after you. A Hope Chocolate makes the fog fade and float away.

  • Move: your mouse or trackpad over the room, the arrow keys, the big buttons, or a joystick or game controller. In the Candy Maze, Clara keeps walking and turns at the next corner you choose.
  • Snowballs throw by themselves (Auto throw in Settings). You can also press the space bar or the Throw button.
  • Pinball flippers: left arrow (or Z) and right arrow (or M), the Left and Right buttons, or click the left or right half of the room. The ball launches by itself, or press the space bar.
  • Paddle Ball two players: the left player uses W and S, the right player uses the arrow keys.

These are original games made for The Woonsocket Wonders.

Good game!
